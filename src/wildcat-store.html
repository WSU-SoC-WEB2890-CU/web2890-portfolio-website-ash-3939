Wildcat Store Website

Project Overview

I was part of the team that helped implement a new website for the Wildcat Store, the university bookstore, using NetSuite. The Wildcat Store serves as an online marketplace for students, faculty, and alumni to purchase textbooks, merchandise, and university-related items. The project aimed to create a seamless, engaging, and user-friendly experience for all visitors. My role involved selecting the website template, customizing the color palette, categorizing items, and resolving coding issues to ensure the website was functional and met design expectations. The overall goal was to design a website that was both visually appealing and easy to navigate for the university community.

Client Overview:
The Wildcat Store is the official university bookstore serving students, faculty, alumni, and the broader university community. Its goal is to provide an efficient and user-friendly online marketplace for textbooks, university apparel, supplies, and other university-related items. The website needed to be visually appealing, easy to navigate, and aligned with the university’s branding. Additionally, it had to support the large volume of customers accessing it, especially during the back-to-school season.

Project Expectations:
The website had to provide a clean, organized product catalog, offer responsive design across all devices, Integrate with NetSuite for product management and cart functionality, and reflect the university’s color palette and branding.

Methods

The development process involved:

  • Template Selection & Customization: I was responsible for evaluating and selecting the template that would best represent the Wildcat Store’s branding and deliver a clean, user-friendly layout. After choosing the template, I worked to customize the layout and make design adjustments to ensure it met our specific needs and preferences.
  • Category Organization & Product Layout: To enhance the browsing experience, I helped define categories for the products, including textbooks, university apparel, and supplies. This involved ensuring the categories were logically structured and clearly labeled to make it easy for users to find items they were looking for. I also worked on ensuring that the products were displayed in an easy-to-read, well-organized format.
  • Color Palette Selection: Since the website represents the university, it was important that the color palette reflected the institution's branding. I carefully selected a color scheme that incorporated the university’s colors, making sure the design was cohesive and visually aligned with the school’s identity. The colors were chosen to create a professional and inviting look while ensuring accessibility and readability.
  • Code Debugging & Error Fixing: During the customization process, I identified a few coding errors that were affecting the site’s functionality and appearance. I worked on fixing these issues, ensuring that the template’s layout was properly displayed, and the website functioned without glitches across all devices.

Tools

The primary tools and technologies used included:

  • Languages & Frameworks: NetSuite platform, HTML, CSS
  • Styling: Custom color palette aligned with the university’s branding and fonts that also align with the brand.
  • Additional Tools: NetSuite platform for product management, testing tools for ensuring responsive design

Users

The website’s primary users include students, faculty, and alumni, who are looking to purchase textbooks, university apparel, and other university-related items. The site needed to cater to a diverse audience, ensuring ease of use across various age groups and technical abilities.

Details and Findings

Design Decisions

  • Template Selection & Customization: The first step was selecting an appropriate website template. I reviewed several options to find a layout that suited the university’s brand while being functional and easy to navigate. After choosing the template, I began customizing it to match the branding guidelines, such as updating the color palette and adjusting the layout. The selected template allowed for flexibility in arranging product categories and displaying featured items, ensuring it would be responsive across all devices.
  • Category Organization: Products were categorized into 8 categories which are: Textbooks, Weber Gear, Tech, Wildcat Spirit, Medical, Supplies, Alumni, and Graduation. This categorization was critical in ensuring a smooth user experience, especially for first-time visitors who may not be familiar with the store’s inventory.
  • Color Palette & Branding:: The color palette was chosen to reflect the university’s official colors, making the website visually cohesive with other university materials. I selected contrasting tones to ensure readability and used softer hues for background elements to avoid overwhelming the user. The design maintained a professional, yet inviting, tone to encourage users to browse and shop comfortably.

Strongest Features

  • Product Catalog & Filtering: A major component of the website is the product catalog, which needed to be easy to browse and search. I added filters by category, price, and availability to help users narrow down their options quickly. This feature improved the overall user experience, especially for those shopping for textbooks in a specific course.
  • NetSuite Integration: NetSuite was integrated to manage the store's products, including inventory management and order processing. I ensured that products and prices were automatically synced with the NetSuite platform, providing real-time updates and reducing the chance of errors.
  • Responsive Design: The website needed to perform seamlessly across different screen sizes. I use custom CSS to ensure that the layout was optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile. This made sure users could shop comfortably regardless of the device they were using.

Problems I Encountered and What I Learned

Challenges

  • Template Customization: After troubleshooting and testing various changes to the HTML and CSS, the template was successfully customized. The final design maintained its functionality while aligning with the university’s branding guidelines, ensuring a cohesive and polished look.
  • Responsive Layout Issues: By refining the CSS media queries, I was able to make the website responsive on all screen sizes. This ensured a smooth user experience on mobile devices and improved product display, meeting the accessibility standards for different device types.
  • NetSuite Integration: Working closely with the NetSuite team and thoroughly studying the integration process allowed me to resolve data syncing issues. This ensured real-time inventory updates and accurate product details, creating a seamless experience for users during checkout.

Solutions and Lessons

  • I gained a deeper understanding of customizing website templates to meet specific branding and functional needs. This included working with HTML and CSS to make targeted adjustments and ensure the design was both appealing and user-friendly.
  • I learned how important it is to properly categorize products for an optimal user experience. Ensuring products were grouped logically and clearly helped users navigate the site more easily, ultimately leading to better customer satisfaction.
  • I enhanced my skills in selecting and applying color palettes that not only represent a brand’s identity but also create an accessible and aesthetically pleasing experience for users.
  • The experience improved my ability to identify, troubleshoot, and fix issues in code, specifically dealing with layout problems and responsiveness across devices. It was a great opportunity to refine my debugging skills and apply them to real-world scenarios.
  • Working with a team to implement a functional, branded online store gave me valuable experience in team-based web development projects. The process of gathering feedback, implementing changes, and seeing the website come to life was both rewarding and educational.

Takeaways
One of the key takeaways was how important it is to make navigation as simple as possible. By clearly categorizing products and integrating filtering options, the website became much easier to navigate, which contributed to an improved shopping experience. I learned how crucial it is to maintain consistent branding throughout the website. Using the university’s official colors and branding elements helped create a professional yet welcoming atmosphere, enhancing user trust and engagement. Testing was an essential part of the process. After initial development, I conducted user testing to gather feedback on the website’s usability and made adjustments based on their input. This included making the cart more visible and streamlining the checkout process.

Results
This project allowed me to enhance my skills in full-stack development, from template customization and UX design to backend integration with NetSuite. By focusing on user experience, responsive design, and seamless integration, we were able to deliver a functional and visually appealing website for the Wildcat Store. The final product provided an efficient and enjoyable shopping experience for users, while also reflecting the university’s branding and values. Despite facing challenges with customization and integration, I was able to overcome these issues and deliver a polished, user-friendly product. This project reinforced the importance of balancing aesthetics with functionality in web design.
